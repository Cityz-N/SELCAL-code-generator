Rules applied
  • Four tones in two pairs, written AB-CD.
  • Standard tones: A B C D E F G H J K L M P Q R S (no I, N or O).
  • No tone may appear more than once in a code.
  • Within each pair, tones are in ascending order (AB valid, BA not).
  • Extended (SELCAL32) adds T U V W X Y Z and 1–9. Extended codes here always contain at least one new tone, so they never overlap the standard set.

For illustration only. Real SELCAL codes are assigned to aircraft by the registrar (Aviation Spectrum Resources Inc.), not self-generated.
